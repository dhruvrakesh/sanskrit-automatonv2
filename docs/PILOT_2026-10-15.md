## 2. PILOT_FIX_2026_10_15

**scripts/scholar_translate.py:**
- **ref_of:** an OCR reading's verse_ref is a number the reading found, not a reference: the Hayaśīrṣa's 20.10 was
  called "4", its sentence "20.5-4", and the text's first verse "2". A verse_ref now counts only where the edition
  numbers its verses (an e-text: etext, gretil, bori), or where it has a dot and the engine is not known; else the
  reference is page.idx. MBh01's references are as before;
- **--fill-engine:** only this engine's own rows count as done, and only they are the context of the verses after. A
  run that stopped goes on where it stopped: no `--redo` (which redoes everything), and no other engine's English as its
  context. latest_rows takes an engine;
- **--compare-every N:** the second judge (gemini-2.5-pro, beside the working English) reads one sentence in N, chosen by
  the hash of its first passage, not by its parity (which sets the order A and B). 1, every one, as before;
- the dry run says both; the NEXT line keeps them.

**scripts/scholar_duel.py:**
- **a span is its verses:** a reading counts as done by its rows, whatever the span was called (the names change with
  ref_of). The page shows one span for the same verses, and `--pick` takes the old name too;
- **the tally** adds "best, its own model left out" for each judge.

**tests/test_scholar_2026_10_14.py:** its .gitignore replay ends at `.gitignore.bak_hygiene_` (REPO_HYGIENE came after
it). That was the one failure on the PC after `88370ae`.

## 3. Your questions

- **Does the hub use the Claude key?** No. Only `scripts/claude_engine.py` reads ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, and it runs only
  when a scholarly run or a head to head names a Claude engine, or for `--probe`.
  - The dashboard, and every job it starts (OCR, vision, the working English and Hindi, the judges, entities,
    embeddings, pictures, the mirror), runs on Gemini as before. infer_mt.py runs gemini: and openai: engines only.
  - Never set MT_ENGINE to an anthropic: engine: infer_mt.py would warn and fall back to echo, so the working English
    would be the Sanskrit itself.
  - The Srangam site calls no model with these keys; it reads the Lovable database.
  - No restart is needed.
- **Should the upserts or the context move to Claude?** No.
  - The mirror (corpus_sync.py to the edge to corpus.*) carries passages and the working English and Hindi; it does
    not depend on the engine.
  - Ask the Corpus searches vectors made by Google's embedding model (build_embeddings.py: gemini-embedding-001, or
    text-embedding-004). One index needs one embedding model, and Anthropic does not offer one ("Anthropic does not
    offer its own embedding model", platform.claude.com, Embeddings). The vectors stay on Gemini.
  - The scholarly English, by whichever engine, reaches the site through S4, after a person accepts its rows; each row
    carries its engine's name.
  - The texts already translated are not redone with Claude: the working layer stays on gemini-2.5-flash, which is
    cheap, and the credit goes to the scholarly layer.
