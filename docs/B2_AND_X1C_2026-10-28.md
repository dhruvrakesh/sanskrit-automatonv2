## 3. "Are the genealogies cutting edge? Is this done?"

Done: G3. The site draws each line as the desk does, every step with what it rests on (the index's words and
references, the GRETIL verse where both names stand with the names marked, the epics' verses counted, our own
passages as links into the reader), other parents with "line through", siblings, children, the credits.

Not cutting edge yet, and said so. It is a clear, accessible ladder with an evidence panel, not a graph. This release
(G3b) adds what was missing for "the inter relationships and the sizeable context":

- **The historian's view (B2):** Raychaudhuri's verdict as a chip on the step ("Raychaudhuri agrees", "another
  relation", "the other way"), and in the evidence his words as the scan reads them, with the page;
- **a strip over the line:** every step at a glance, coloured by the best of what it rests on, one click to choose it;
- **"In our corpus":** our names on the line, how often our texts name the person and in how many texts, the names
  named with it in our passages (said not to be a kinship), and what the historian says of the name;
- **the genealogies page** lists the work and its claims; **a name's page** says what the historian says of it.

What would make it cutting edge, each priced and gated (section 8): G3c, an interactive graph of a line and its
branches (a layout library loaded only on that page); G2, the Professor's commentary on a whole line, cited and
marked as a reading (metered); G4, the genealogical chapters translated so our names reach more of the indexes'
persons (about $24 for the first slice).

## 6. The documentation, mapped

`python scripts\docs_map.py` prints what docs\DOCS_MAP.md would be; `--write` writes it; `--check` exits 1 when it is
stale (the release can run it). It reads only the files' own first lines: each dated file's title and markers, grouped
by what it is about (start here; plans and paths; texts, scans and repair; translation and its quality; names,
relations and genealogies; the site and the mirror; the desk), newest first, and the cloud SQL in the order it is
pasted, each with what it needs. Nothing in it is written by hand, so it cannot drift from the files.
