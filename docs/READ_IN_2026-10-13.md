## 2. READ_IN_2026_10_13

- **A scan is read when vision has read 80% of its pages and 95% are merged.** Then its NEXT is the in-place
  re-ingest of the pages read (`reingest_pages.py`). The pages vision may still read come after, as an ALSO with a
  cap that fits them. The others keep Tesseract's reading.
- **The pages vision failed on twice are not pages to read.** They are counted from the ledger and left out of the
  estimate. A scan whose every remaining page failed twice is "find a source".
- **The drift check gives the in-place re-ingest first.** The whole text again (wipe and re-ingest) comes second,
  with what it deletes said.
- **On the live figures,** the Natyasastra moves from *repair the scan* to *use the repair*: 505 pages go in, free,
  with nothing to lose (0 English). 11 pages remain for vision, about $0.08.
