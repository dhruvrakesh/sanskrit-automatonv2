## 2. How they join: the thread a verse follows

**Down, from the source to the reader:**
1. **The source:** a scan in `inbox\` (page PDFs), or a typed e-text (GRETIL, BORI, sanskritdocuments.org) read by
   `etext_witness.py` as a witness, `<doc>__w_<site>` (docs/ETEXT_WITNESS_2026-10-11.md). archive.org is searched by the
   source finder; vidyut-data checks words and metre.
2. **The reading:** Tesseract, Gemini vision where the scan is poor, the repair path and the re-ingest of changed
   pages only (docs/REPAIR_PATH_2026-10-10.md, docs/REINGEST_2026-10-12.md). Each passage keeps its page, its position
   and, from an e-text, its own verse reference.
3. **The working English and Hindi** (`passages.translation`, `translations_l10n`): gemini-2.5-flash by default
   (`MT_ENGINE`), Sonnet in one pass text by text (Tier R; T2 for marked verses). Old English goes to
   `translation_history`; each row names its engine.
4. **The checks:** the screen (Claude Haiku on Flash's English, calibrated 17 Oct), Gemini on Claude's, your verdicts
   on the desk (docs/JUDGE_SCREEN_2026-10-17.md, docs/TRANSLATION_DESK_2026-10-17.md).
5. **The scholarly English** (`scholar_translations`): Sonnet with a critic and revisions, Opus's audit, Gemini's judge,
   notes and source doubts; each row tied to the Sanskrit it was made from (stale when that changes)
   (docs/SCHOLAR_V2_2026-10-14.md, docs/SOP_TRANSLATION_2026-10-17.md).
6. **The editions:** `export_html.py` (working, `--scholar`, `--hindi`), `scholar_translate.py --report` (prose), the
   Booksmith's PDFs (docs/EDITIONS_2026-10-17.md).
7. **The mirror:** `corpus_sync.py` upserts what changed (digests, docs/cloud/C4b) through the `corpus-ingest` edge
   function into the site's `corpus` schema, with `CORPUS_SYNC_SECRET` (in `.env` and in Lovable Secrets only). The
   register (`data\source_verdicts.jsonl`) keeps held, retired and witness texts at home (docs/CORPUS_MIRROR_2026-10-08.md,
   docs/MIRROR_CAP_2026-10-12.md, docs/PATH_2026-10-12.md).
8. **The site:** the reader (C5, C5b, C17), the library (C6), roles (C7), media (C8), search by meaning
   (`embed-published-passages`, `search-corpus`, `search-texts`: C1 to C3), the Researchers' Corner (C9 to C13,
   `corpus-desk`, `corner-mail` with `RESEND_API_KEY` in Lovable Secrets only), Learn (C11). The SQL is in
   `docs/cloud/` and is pasted in the Lovable Cloud SQL editor only, one block a paste, read-only checks before and after.

**Up, from the reader to the workshop:** a researcher's request in the Corner (answered on the PC by
`corner_worker.py`), your blind reading (BM2: it chose the working English), your verdicts on the desk, a flagged verse,
the spend meter (`usage_meter.py`; docs/COST_TRUTH_2026-10-15.md), and failures (the judge's quota of 17 Oct) each change
what the workshop does next.

## 4. Engines and rules (the SOP, as it stands)

- `MT_ENGINE` stays `gemini:gemini-2.5-flash`; never an `anthropic:` engine there. Sonnet in one pass is chosen per
  job (Tier R, T2), as BM1, BM3 and BM2 agreed (docs/BM2_FIRST_READING_2026-10-17.md).
- A judge is of the other family: Gemini on Claude's English, Haiku on Flash's. A screen is calibrated before it is
  used, by a rule written down first.
- Fix by cause: Sonnet where the English is at fault, a better source where the Sanskrit is. A whole text again only
  when a fifth or more of it is marked.
- The scholarly pipeline is for editions (its notes are what it adds: docs/BM3_CHECK_2026-10-17.md); a canary of 20
  before every run; one Gemini job at a time.
- Backups first (`db_backup.py`); never VACUUM; never open `data\context.db` from the VM; never hand-edit
  `translations_l10n`; one dashboard, restarted only when idle.
