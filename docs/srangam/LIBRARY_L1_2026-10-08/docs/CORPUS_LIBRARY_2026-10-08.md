# The library over the working corpus (2026-10-08)

Marker: CORPUS_LIBRARY_C6_2026_10_08. This brings the translation desk's own library pages to the site, for signed-in readers: the Shelf, Stories and the names, from the dashboard at 127.0.0.1:5057. They are built on the mirror the desk keeps current every two hours.

## Pages

| Page | What a reader gets | From the desk's |
|---|---|---|
| `/corpus` (Library) | **Shelves.** Every text on its shelf: Vedas, Vedāṅgas, Purāṇas, and so on. **Titles.** The confirmed title, or the one derived from the code, in italics and labelled as derived. **Each card.** Its series ("Hārīta Saṃhitā · 3"), English and Hindi coverage bars, and the pipeline stages, 13 dots with the desk's reason for each as a tooltip and in a popover. **Stories.** The text's stories. **Tools.** A filter that ignores diacritics, four sorts, "with stories" and "published". | Shelf page (`library_web.py`, `configs/collections.json`), `doc_stage` |
| `/corpus/stories` | **The list.** Stories drawn from the texts, one text at a time. **Visibility.** Readers see approved stories only; editors also see drafts and candidates, marked as such. | Stories page (`stories_web.py`) |
| `/corpus/stories/:doc/:id` | The Sanskrit line it turns on (linked to the text), the story in English and Hindi side by side, "why this episode", every cited passage as a link, the check against its citations, and the previous and next story. | `stories.py` (verify, cites) |
| `/corpus/names` | **The index.** The names index: people, deities, places, peoples, rivers and mountains. **Counts.** How many passages and how many texts name each. **Search.** By name or variant, a kind filter, and paging. | `entities`, `entity_mentions` |
| `/corpus/names/:name` | Every passage that names it, in reading order, with the spelling used there and a link to the passage. | the same |
| `/corpus/:doc` | **Name chips.** The names in each passage, as chips. Each opens a card (kind, note, spelling) with a link to every passage that names it. A "Names" toggle in the reading bar turns them off. **Links by reference.** `?at=18.2` opens the reader page that holds scan page 18, at passage 18.2. Story citations use it. | reader name highlights |

## Data

- **Shelves.** `src/data/corpusShelf.json` is generated by the automaton's `scripts/emit_corpus_shelf.py` from `configs/collections.json`.
  - It carries each member's derived title and series.
  - Do not edit it here. Run the script again when the shelves change.
- **Everything else.** It comes from five database functions in `docs/cloud/C6_corpus_library_2026-10-08.sql`, in the automaton repo: `corpus_reader_progress`, `corpus_reader_stories`, `corpus_reader_names`, `corpus_reader_name` and `corpus_reader_page_names`.
  - They have the same gate as C5. Only signed-in callers may execute them.
  - Stories: approved for readers, all but retired and rejected for admins.
  - On a test set of 8,000 names and 32,000 mentions: 27 ms, 21 ms and 4 ms.
- **Code.** `src/lib/corpusLibrary.ts` holds the loaders and the pure rules. A function the database does not have yet is `missing`, and the page leaves that part out quietly.
