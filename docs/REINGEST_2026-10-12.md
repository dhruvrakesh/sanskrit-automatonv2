## 3. What is built

**`scripts/reingest_pages.py` (R3):**
- **It works page by page.** It cuts each page of `data\raw_merged` as the ingest would
  (`ingest_jsonl_fast.upsert_passages` on a scratch table), compares it with the database, and changes
  only the pages that differ.
- **On a changed page:**
  - a passage whose text is unchanged keeps its row, its English, Hindi, vector, reviews and history;
  - if its place moved, a picture or a story anchored to it moves too;
  - a passage whose text changed or went is deleted with what hangs on it, written first to
    `data\reingest\<doc>_<stamp>.jsonl`;
  - new passages come in untranslated;
  - a blank page loses its passages.
- **The result is what a full re-ingest makes.** It leaves the same passages, column for column
  (tested), without losing the rest.
- **The safeguards:**
  - the plan is free and read-only;
  - `--apply` makes a backup first and changes one transaction;
  - it checks that the database is still what it planned on;
  - a lost translation, picture or story must be said with `--replace N`;
  - it is refused while the dashboard runs a database writer;
  - then come `classify_doc.py` and the drift check;
  - the register is untouched;
  - the site gets the changed passages at the next mirror run.
- **Measured on the 11 Oct 22:49 backup** (read immutable) against today's repaired pages:
  - the Tantric Texts: 7 pages change, 145 garbage passages go and 3 vision passages come in;
  - Karan: 7 pages, 91 go and 6 come in, and none of its 13 pictures is on those pages;
  - Mallapurana and the Shatapatha: no page changes.

**`scripts/judge_review.py`** (read-only):
- the judge's figures, text by text;
- with `--doc`, the passages it marks, with its reason, the Sanskrit and the English; a reason that
  blames the Sanskrit is counted apart;
- `--html` writes the same as a page to read.

**`scripts/patch_reingest_2026_10_12.py`:**
- `merge_ocr_sources.py`: the same pattern, with no warning.
- `repair_text.py`:
  - vision is priced from what it cost (usage_log, by text). It had said "269 pages, about $0.11"
    for the Natyasastra, whose pages cost $0.0111 each, so about $2.99;
  - where `--use-repaired` would replace translations or pictures, it offers `reingest_pages.py`.
- `benchmark_sources.py`: the judge's line says how many passages have a wrong meaning, and the level
  (material from 10%).
- `dashboard.py` (on a restart):
  - "Plan: only the changed pages" and "Re-ingest the changed pages" on the repair page;
  - *Use the repaired text* sends `--replace 0`;
  - `/benchmarks` shows the judge.
- `.gitignore`: `data/reingest/` stays local.
