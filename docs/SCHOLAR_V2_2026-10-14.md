## 2. Why MBh01's Hindi shows "Q:53%"

The Q is the reading's quality score (`passages.quality_score`), not the Hindi's. It was made for OCR:

    0.6 x (the share of Devanagari signs) + 0.4 x min(1, dandas / 5)

The BORI e-text writes its verses without dandas, so an e-text scores 0.6 at most: MBh01 has 0.48 to 0.58 (the mean
0.52). Since then `ingest_jsonl_fast.py` gives a GRETIL record 0.98, but MBh01 went in before that, with no engine
recorded.

It does not change what is translated: the bar is 0.35. It does change what readers see:
- the dashboard shows Q:53%;
- the Srangam reader shows "OCR confidence 52%", and on the 267 verses below 0.5 "the printed page was hard to read, so
  the Sanskrit may hold misreadings". For the critical edition's own e-text, that is wrong.

`heal_etext_meta.py` mends it (section 3).

## 3. What this release does

### SCHOLAR_V2_2026_10_14 (`scholar-v2`)

**Sentences (`--groups`, on by default):**
- one call (`scholar-groups-v1`, gemini-2.5-pro) reads up to 40 verses that follow one another and says where each
  Sanskrit sentence ends: at most 6 verses a sentence, a speaker's line with its verse, most sentences a verse;
- a sentence over several verses is drafted as one passage of English, in the order English needs, read by the critic
  and judged as one. The draft gives it cut at the verse boundaries ("segments"), and says what it moved;
- stored verse by verse: each verse's part in `translation`, the whole in `group_translation`, and `group_ref` (for
  example 1.1.15-1.1.19). The cost is shared among the verses;
- an answer that does not fit (a verse missing or out of order, over 6 verses) is not used: each verse stands alone. A
  draft whose segments do not fit keeps the whole passage on the first verse.
- `--no-groups`: every verse alone, as before.

**The second judge (`--compare`, on by default; `scholar-compare-v1`, gemini-2.5-pro):**
- it reads the scholarly and the working English side by side, with the verse's context, in an order it is not told
  (the passage id decides which is A);
- it gives each its fidelity and its errors (major or minor), and says which is the more faithful;
- a major error it finds in the scholarly English flags the verse: a person reads it;
- stored as `compare` and `compare_better` (scholarly, equal or working). The flash judge stays, for its scores.

**The prompt (`scholar-v2`, `scholar-critic-v2`):**
- notes: three a verse at most; none for a common word (king, sage, great-souled, lord) or one that repeats the
  translation; the Sanskrit in a note spelt as the IAST given is;
- a speaker's line opens the translation ("The sūta said:") without its own " //";
- the critic reads the notes too: a wrong fact or a misspelt Sanskrit word is an issue.

**The price:**
- this prompt version's own mean once there are 10 rows of it;
- before that, scholar-v1's measured mean, with a compare call at half of it: about $0.015 a verse.

**The pages:**
- `/scholar/<doc>`:
  - a sentence over verses is one block, the Sanskrit of each verse beside the English, with the verse numbers in it;
  - the second judge's verdict on each verse;
  - the earlier version under the fold (the pilot's scholar-v1 rows stay, and a v2 run makes version 2);
  - a summary: the second judge's count of "more faithful", "as faithful", "less";
- `/scholar/<doc>/prose`, and `--report` beside the verses (`exports\scholar_<doc>_prose.html`):
  - the text read as a book reads, chapter by chapter;
  - the verse numbers small, a new paragraph where a speaker begins;
  - the notes at each chapter's end;
  - a flagged passage not yet accepted is underlined;
  - a stale one (its Sanskrit changed) is left out.
- New columns (`group_ref`, `group_translation`, `moved`, `compare`, `compare_better`) are added in place at the next
  `--yes`, `--review` or `--term`. The pages read a table without them.

**The importer:** `import_mbh_gretil.py --src` with a file that is not there says so, not a traceback.

### ETEXT_META_2026_10_14 (`scripts/heal_etext_meta.py`)

**What it sets.** For the passages whose page file (`data/raw/<doc>_NNNN.jsonl`) has an e-text record with the same
verse and the same text (normalized as the ingest normalizes):
- `ocr_engine`: the record's engine, where it is empty;
- `quality_score`: 0.98, the ingest's e-text value, where it is lower.

Nothing else: no text and no translation.

**How it runs:**
- a plan by default;
- `--apply`: refused while the dashboard runs a job that changes the text (a Hindi job does not stop it), then a
  backup, then one transaction. The values before go to `data/heal/etext_meta_<doc>_<stamp>.jsonl`, and `--undo`
  puts them back.

**On MBh01's first three chapters (the backup of 14 Oct):** all 496 passages match their records. The site gets the
change at the next mirror run.
