## 2. The rules (`text_path.py`, decide())

In order; the first that applies decides.

| # | When | The path | Next |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | accepted as complete | done | - |
| 2 | another text holds the same work, read by vision (code suffix, 50%+ of letter groups shared) | retire | `retire_doc.py --dry-run`, then `--yes` |
| 3 | in repair | in repair | the repair into the database first if it is not all in; translate; `--release` |
| 4 | an e-text witness (here only) | in repair | translate; `--release` adopts it |
| 5 | set aside or withdrawn | find a source | `--find-source`; `--restore` |
| 6 | no passages | ingest (or retire an empty record) | `/api/ocr` or `/api/ingest` |
| 7 | Tesseract read under 90% of the page PDFs | ingest | `/api/ocr`, then `reingest_pages.py --merged-dir data\raw` |
| 8 | under 80% of the pages read are in the database | ingest | `reingest_pages.py --merged-dir data\raw` (plan, then `--apply`) |
| 9 | Tesseract's text, vision has read 80%+ and the repair is merged | use the repair | merge `--apply`; `reingest_pages.py` plan, `--apply --replace N` |
| 10 | Tesseract's text, its scan here, damaged or not translated | repair the scan | `repair_text.py --repair --max-usd X`, then the re-ingest |
| 11 | Tesseract's text, damaged, no scan | find a source | `--find-source`; `--withdraw` offered at 5%+ of letters misread (or a quarter of a judged sample wrong) |
| 12 | untranslated, or Hindi behind | translate | the repair's changed pages first, if any |
| 13 | translated, never judged | judge a sample | `judge_sample.py --limit 40` (dry run, then `--yes`) |
| 14 | the judge finds a wrong meaning in 10%+ | translate again | `judge_review.py`, then T2 |
| 15 | otherwise | done | - |

- **A repair goes in before the translation.** Where the repaired text differs from the database on some
  pages (`reingest_pages.plan`), the re-ingest of those pages is the first NEXT, in repair and translate too,
  with `--replace N` as the plan would ask. Otherwise the English is made from the old reading.
- **Prices.** A text with 20 or more vision pages of its own in usage_log is priced from them; the others at
  the corpus price, with the dearest text's price beside it as the upper bound. The cap (`--max-usd`) is the
  estimate plus a quarter, at least $1; a run that reaches it stops before spending more, and its first pages
  measure the text's own price.

## 4. PATH_2026_10_12

- **`scripts/text_path.py`** (new, read-only): the chart above, for every text in the database, the inbox and
  `data/raw`; `--doc X` with every fact; `--html`; writes `data/text_path.json` for the dashboard.
- **`ocr_consensus.py`:** a page vision fails on is written down (`data/ocr_consensus/<doc>_vision_failed.json`).
  It is tried after the others; its failures do not count towards the five in a row that stop a run; after two
  failed runs it is left to Tesseract (`--retry-refused` tries it again). The ledger is new, so on the first
  run a page below the last page vision read that has no reading is written down once (the runs go in page
  order: it failed before); the Natyasastra's 24 such pages (below page 292, its last read) go last at once. The spend cap is asked before each
  page and after a failure (`ocr_vision.py` exits 0 with nothing when the cap refuses it), so a cap reached
  stops the run as such and marks no page.
- **`repair_text.py`:** the repair's NEXT gives a cap the estimate fits under. `--withdraw REASON`: a new state.
  The text is taken off the site and out of Ask the Corpus, and kept here, until a better source replaces it;
  `--restore` undoes it.
- **`corpus_sync.py`:** a withdrawn text is not live. A full run (not `--doc`) retires its rows on the site, as
  for a retired text; the guard on many texts gone at once holds. Restored, the next run sends it back (the
  ingest clears `retired_at` on a re-send).
- **`dashboard.py`** (on a restart): `/path`, the chart, with *Chart the path again* (free, a few minutes);
  *Withdraw it from the site* where the repair page advises it; withdrawn texts left out of Ask the Corpus
  (words and meaning); links to `/path` from the Library, `/sources` and `/benchmarks`.
- **`.gitignore`:** `data/text_path.json`.

**Tests:** `tests/test_path_2026_10_12.py` (28); the patcher replays in `tests/test_reingest_2026_10_12.py`,
`tests/test_quality_2026_10_11.py`, `tests/test_etext_witness_2026_10_11.py` and
`tests/test_repair_heal_2026_10_11.py` compare with this patch's backups. The full suite in the sandbox: the
same 69 failures and errors as before the patch (none new; they need the machine's packages and files).
