"Held" is `translate_passages.py`'s debris guard (TRANSLATE_DEBRIS_GUARD_2026_10_04). The text's
Tesseract OCR carries debris and its page PDFs are in the inbox, so the guard holds the text until OCR
consensus has repaired the source.

The console said none of this, for four reasons:

1. **A running job showed no output.** `_run_job` used `communicate()`, so a job's output reached
   `/api/job` only when the job ended.
2. **Jobs started outside the page were never followed.** A job started in the Library, in another
   tab, or before the page loaded got a badge, but no lines in the Log and no outcome. Its badge said
   *running* until the page was reloaded.
3. **A run that started and ended between two looks never appeared.** A hold takes 0.3 s.
4. **The Log knew only [OK] and [FAIL].** A hold read as a failure, and "nothing to do" read as
   success.

Two more faults:
- **The Log stopped showing new lines once a job had written 6,000 characters.** It compared the
  length of a 6,000-character window.
- **The Live tab showed NaN.** `started_at` carries `+00:00`, and the page appended `Z`.

The Library's *queued* (with a check mark) is the dashboard's code from before DESK_HEAL. The new Library notes and
follow-up come only when the dashboard is restarted. The restart was refused because the Yoga Vasistha
OCR was running: page 766 of 1,982 at 16:31, about 54 pages an hour.

## 3. What changed

### LIVE_LOG_2026_10_10 (`scripts\patch_live_log_2026_10_10.py`, needs DESK_HEAL)

**`dashboard.py`**, on a restart:
- `_run_job` reads the job's two pipes line by line while it runs (two readers, as `communicate()`
  has, so neither pipe fills).
- `/api/job` shows the last 200 lines as they come.
- Every line is still kept for the end.
- A job stopped by hand keeps what it wrote.

**`dashboard_static.html`**, on a reload:
- **The Log follows every job, wherever it was started.** A job found running is adopted by its id:
  its lines, its outcome, and its badge ends with it.
- **Outcomes say what they are:**
  - *[HELD]*, with the text's OCR-consensus command;
  - *[NOTHING]*;
  - *[OK]*, with N of M translated and how many were below the OCR quality bar;
  - *[STOPPED]*;
  - *[FAIL]*.
- **A held badge is not red.**
- **Short runs are caught.** Runs that start and end between two looks come from the job history
  (`/api/jobs/history`, every 8 s while the page is visible), with a toast.
- **The Log opens with the last five runs** instead of *Awaiting jobs...*.
- **New lines keep coming past 6,000 characters.** The last line shown marks the place.
- **The Live tab shows how long a run has taken.**

### HUB_V2_1_2026_10_10 (`scripts\patch_hub_v2_1_2026_10_10.py`, over v2)

The Translation engine card shows:
- **the last runs and what each did**, from `data\jobs.jsonl`, with today's totals;
- **the texts held for an OCR repair**;
- **how far a running OCR has got**: its page files in `data\raw` against its page PDFs in the inbox,
  counted at most once a minute.

The page asks `/api/online` every 30 s, and only after its first look at the services.

**Re-ingesting a repaired text is a decision, not a chore.**
- `ocr_consensus.py` prints the steps: back up, `wipe_doc.py`, `ingest_jsonl_fast.py` from
  `data\raw_merged`, classify, translate, QA.
- `wipe_doc.py` deletes the text's passages and, in its own words, "translations are deleted with the
  passages".
- The Hindi rows, the vectors and the picture anchors point at the old passages.
- Translating again costs nothing where a passage's text did not change (`mt_cache`). It costs where
  the text changed.

For Natyasastra and the Tantric Texts there is nothing to lose: no English to speak of, no Hindi, no
pictures. They are the ones to repair first. For Karan Aagama and Hayashirsha the choice is between:
- repairing, which brings better source text but replaces existing translations; and
- translating what is left anyway with `--allow-debris`, for that text only.

     translates as before.
