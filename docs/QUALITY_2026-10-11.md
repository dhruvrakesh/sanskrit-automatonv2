## 3. What is built

- **`merge_ocr_sources.py`:**
  - **Tesseract is a fallback only where it read text**: Devanagari (half the letters or more, and
    not fragments among marks) or English (words of 3.5 letters or more, under 10% stray marks).
    These thresholds are calibrated on the 678 fallback pages then in the corpus.
  - **On garbage:** vision's line is kept, or, when vision found nothing, the page is written
    **blank** (engine `blank`, empty text). Nothing false goes in.
  - **Variants** are not recorded against garbage.
  - **The report** names every page not simply taken from vision, and why.
- **`ocr_consensus.py`:** the drift check counts a blank page current when the database has nothing
  for it, and stale when it still holds the garbage.
- **`etext_witness.py`:**
  - `quality()` measures glued, malformed, stray marks and plausibility (above);
  - the preview prints the witness's quality beside the scan's;
  - **a witness no cleaner than Tesseract is refused** (noise over 3% of its words) unless
    `--force`, and between 1.5% and 3% it is shown with a warning;
  - a scan too damaged to have readable pages is measured by all its pages;
  - `witness.json` and `data/witnesses.jsonl` carry the noise;
  - `dsbcproject.org` is an e-text host for `--url`.
- **`benchmark_sources.py`:**
  - every reading gets its noise, glued share and plausibility;
  - **archive.org's OCR is compared by all three measures:**
    - "make it a witness" only when it is cleaner and its own noise is at most 1.5%;
    - "neither is clean" when both are damaged;
  - "the repair misreads more than vision alone" names its Tesseract pages;
  - the glued line names the letter, most often "V";
  - the English made from passages with noise in a tenth of their words or more is counted;
  - **every translation line says the QA is structural** and whether an LLM judge has read it.
- **`source_desk.py`:**
  - it loads `.env`, so `--ai` finds the key;
  - an archive.org error is a verdict with a next step (`--kind pdf`), not a traceback;
  - it links the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon.
- **`dashboard.py`** (after a restart): Noise and Plausible columns on the repair page's benchmark,
  and "misreads / noise" on `/benchmarks`.

**Nothing in the database changes** until a text is merged again and its repaired text is used.
Shatapatha and Mallapurana are translated, so their small gain waits for R3, the in-place
re-ingest.
