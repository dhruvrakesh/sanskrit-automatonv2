## 2. What the measurements changed

- **archive.org's OCR is Tesseract** (archive.org/developers/ocr.html: the change to Tesseract was completed at the end
  of 2020). Its text of our own Satapatha scan (DLI 2015.405693): own misreadings 25.2 in 100 against ours 3.34; it
  agrees with GRETIL in 57.35% where found, and finds 383 of 793 kandikas. It makes Tesseract's mistakes, so it is no
  second witness of a scan we already hold: a source search candidate from archive.org or DLI is a new *scan*, not a
  better *text*, unless its own letters say otherwise.
- **Our vision text** (Gemini 2.5 Flash) misreads 3.58 (the Markandeya) and 3.62 (the Satapatha's kanda 14) aksharas
  in 100 of its own; Tesseract on the same pages 18.15 and 24.49. Triangulated on the Markandeya (the reference our
  text; the voters Tesseract and GRETIL, independent), 2.74 in 100 of our aksharas are outvoted: the mends to read.
- **Engines elsewhere** (arXiv:2606.29213, June 2026, real printed Devanagari, chrF++): Gemini 86.3, Claude 82.2,
  Mistral 77.6, Qwen3-VL-8B 75.2, GPT-5.5 58.5, EasyOCR 58.3, olmOCR 40.5, DeepSeek-OCR 10.4; look-alike letters (ba
  and va, da and dha) are confused across systems. That is why independence is measured, not assumed.
