# The Corner, guided: the yajna of the texts (2026-10-10)

Markers: CORNER_UX_U1_2026_10_10 (the site), CORNER_C13_2026_10_10 (the database).

The working corpus exists so that the texts travel: from the scans, through the desk's translations, to stories, pictures, graphic novels and anthologies that people read. The Researchers' Corner is where invited researchers make that happen. This release makes the Corner lead them to it, one choice at a time, without getting in their way. It also mends one gap in the database that kept researchers from the work the Corner promised them.

## 1. What we found (10 Oct, live)

**On the page (Ask the desk):**
- The Corner showed eleven forms at once, before a text was chosen. Each said "Choose a text first."
- All 51 texts with English were in one list, by title, with no sign of which ones still had no story.
- A story's passages had to be typed as numbers. The placeholders (25.2, 25.9, 25.7) looked like values already filled in.
- After a request, nothing pointed onward: not to the passages, not to Learn.
- In Settings, Reply-to showed desk@nartiang.org as its placeholder. It looked saved. And nartiang.org has no MX record, so no reply sent there can arrive.

**In the database (the gap):**
- `corner._clean` (C9, C10b) accepts these requests from a researcher:
  - `story_write` for a proposed episode or a draft;
  - `story_illustrate` for a draft;
  - `story_edit` for a proposed episode or a draft;
  - `novel_cast` and `novel_draw` for a novel still being drawn.
- The reader functions showed proposed episodes, drafts and novels in progress to editors only:
  - `corpus_reader_stories` (C6) checks `has_role admin`;
  - `corpus_reader_media`, `corpus_reader_novels`, `corpus_reader_novel` and `corpus_reader_media_file` (C8) check `corpus._is_editor()`.
- So, for Kanika and Parth:
  - "Write a proposed episode" always said "No proposed episode in this text";
  - the links on their own finished requests led to a draft they could not open;
  - "Draw the cast" and "Draw the pages" listed none of their own novels.
- This broke what section 1 of `docs/RESEARCHERS_CORNER_2026-10-09.md` and the Researcher Guide tell them they can do.

## 2. The principles

- **Guide, never push.** No pop-ups, no tours to click through, no blocking steps. Every hint is a line or a link that can be ignored.
- **One choice at a time.** First the text, then what to make, then the one form for it.
- **Show what is ready.** The page says what can be made from this text now, from what it has already loaded.
- **Never spend on the researcher's behalf.** A suggestion only fills a form. The desk is asked only by the form's own "Ask the desk", at the estimate it shows, and a researcher's paid request still waits for an editor.
- **Shared, not competitive.** "The offering so far" is the team's: no names, no ranks, as in Learn (no leaderboard).
- **The database decides.** The guide shows only what the database returns to this viewer, and offers a kind only when `corner_kinds()` lets this viewer ask for it.
- **Light.** The guide adds no question to the database on page load except one small row (`corner_offering()`). The passage picker loads only when opened.

**U2, sparks across the corpus.** One read-only function, `corner_sparks(k)`, for the Corner's people:
- the passages richest in names (from `corpus.mentions`) that no story covers yet;
- proposed episodes that have waited more than seven days to be written;
- approved stories with no picture, and approved stories with no graphic novel, across every text.

Its rows are shown on Ask the desk before a text is chosen, each with "Set it up".

It also adds a team goal for the week, set by the super admin in Settings (for example "five texts get their first story"), and shown as one shared line of progress. And a mark on each text that got its first story this month ("first light").

**U3, Learn T2.**
- Quests for C10's kinds: ideas for pictures, draw an idea, edit, check again.
- "Light the first lamp": a story for a text that had none.
- "Bring a text to life": a story, its picture and a graphic novel from one text.
- After a request, the XP it earned, in a line (never a pop-up).

**U4, sharing (P2, your decision).**
- A public page for a published anthology, with a preview card made from its cover, so that a link sent on WhatsApp looks like what it is.
- The anthology as a printed booklet.
- "A story of the week" on the public home page.
- A story read aloud, through the site's existing text-to-speech functions.

**U5, editors.**
- The queue sorted by what moves the corpus most: a text's first story first.
- Approving a batch of checked drafts.
- "What changed since you last looked."

## 4. U1: what the site does now (Srangam, CORNER_UX_U1_2026_10_10)

**Ask the desk:**
- **The offering so far,** one quiet line under a small flame. It gives texts in English, passages translated, stories told, pictures, graphic novels, anthologies published, requests done this week, and how many texts wait for their first story.
  - The figures come from `corner_offering()` once C13 is in; before C13, from the texts alone.
  - The texts waiting are counted from the texts, as the list and "Where to begin" count them, so the three agree.
  - On a phone it shows the first three figures.
- **Before a text is chosen,** no forms. Instead, "Where to begin":
  - the texts with English and no story yet, the most English first;
  - "Continue with" the text chosen last time on this browser (offered, never chosen for you);
  - "Surprise me".
- **The list of texts** is grouped: "Waiting for their first story", then "With stories".
- **After a text is chosen:**
  - what it has: Hindi, stories by status, pictures, graphic novels;
  - links to read it, its stories, its pictures;
  - "What would you like to make?": a story, a picture, a graphic novel or everything, with how many are ready in each, and a link to the anthologies. The address can carry `?goal=`; a link with `?kind=` (Learn's quests, the next steps of a request) opens on that kind's goal.
- **Ready in this text** lists what can be asked for now, at most four, each with its estimate and "Set it up":
  - find its episodes (the text has no story of any status);
  - write a proposed episode;
  - a picture for a story that has none;
  - a graphic novel of an approved story that has none;
  - ideas for its first pictures.
  - The picture, novel and ideas suggestions appear only for a viewer shown the work in progress: an editor, or a researcher once C13 is in. Before that, "no picture" may only mean "none approved yet".
- **"Set it up"** fills the form, opens the goal, scrolls to the form and puts the cursor in it. It asks nothing.
- **Choosing a goal** hides the other forms without clearing them: a note typed there, or a request's answer, is still there when that goal is shown again.
- **Passages chosen in the text.** "Choose them in the text" (a story) and "Choose it in the text" (a picture) open the text a reader page at a time. They use the reader's own query, so a page read there is not asked for again.
  - Each passage shows its reference and its first English words.
  - "From here", "To here" and "This one" fill the reference.
  - Only a passage the desk takes can be chosen: translated, and not a running head or front matter, as `corner._passage_ok` requires.
  - The range's length is shown, and more than 60 is flagged before the desk is asked.
  - A picture's brief starts from the passage's English, and follows the passage chosen until you write in it.
  - Choosing another text clears the passages chosen.
- The placeholders read "such as 25.2".
- After a request, a line says it counts in Learn. An empty My requests points to Learn too.

**Settings (super admin):**
- Reply-to's placeholder is "an inbox you read".
- Its hint says why an address at nartiang.org loses every reply.
- The site address's placeholder says "such as".

**Anthologies:** a researcher's anthology editor offers approved stories only. `corner_collection_save` refuses any other story from her, and C13 would otherwise show her drafts there. Editors see drafts too, as before.

**Files:** all in `docs/srangam/CORNER_UX_U1_2026-10-10/`, put in by `scripts/patch_srangam_corner_ux_u1_2026_10_10.py`.

## 5. C13: researchers see the work in progress; the offering so far

**What it adds:**
- `corpus._sees_drafts()`: true for an editor, and for anyone the Corner lets ask (`corner._can_request()`: invited researchers and editors). The owner's only.
- `public.corner_offering()`: one row for the strip. For the Corner's people (`corner._gate()`), granted to authenticated.

**What it changes:** the five reader functions see drafts through the helper instead of the editor check. Nothing else changes in them.
- Each is rewritten from its own live definition (`pg_get_functiondef`).
- Exactly one line is changed, and a marker comment is put after it.
- If that line is not there exactly once, nothing at all is changed: the paste stops, naming the function.
- Owners, grants, SECURITY DEFINER and search_path are kept.

**Who sees what afterwards:**
- A reader who is not invited (the reader modes 'readers' and 'signed_in') sees what she saw: approved work only.
- Retired and rejected work stays hidden from everyone.
- A researcher whose role is taken away sees approved work only again.

**Re-running and undoing:**
- Re-running C13 changes nothing.
- A re-run of C6 or C8 puts their own functions back: run C13 again after one.
- The rollback is in C13's header, one paste.

**Checks:** `docs/cloud/C13_checks_2026-10-10.sql`.
- P1 before; V1-V3 after.
- D1: the strip's numbers, straight from the tables.
- D2: the email settings as saved, Reply-to included. The site never shows Reply-to back.

**Tests:** `tests/test_corner_drafts_pg_2026_10_10.py` (6) runs against PostgreSQL with every file the live database has, in order, then C13 twice. It covers:
- the rewrite, and that the grants are kept;
- who sees what, for anon, a reader, a listed reader, a researcher, an admin and the super admin;
- the offering;
- that a researcher's anthology still holds approved stories only;
- the checks' own queries;
- a third run;
- the rollback;
- a function changed since: C13 refuses it and changes nothing.

**The site with C13 and without it:**
- U1 works either way.
- Without C13, the strip is made from the texts, and a researcher's "Ready in this text" never offers a proposed episode, since she cannot see one.
