## 3. What it shows

- **The scholarly arm's lead over Sonnet alone was its notes.** With them shown, both judges preferred it in four
  units in five; without them, it is a coin toss (0.51, 0.52; both intervals hold 0.5). The English itself of the
  scholarly pipeline and of Sonnet in one pass are, to these judges, as good as each other.
- **Both are clearly ahead of flash, the working English,** with or without the notes (0.68 to 0.79), and flash makes
  the most major errors of our arms (3.8 to 4.5 per ten verses).
- **The notes are what the scholarly pipeline adds that a judge values,** and fewer major errors by gemini-2.5-pro
  (0.24 against Sonnet's 1.90; Opus counts them equal, 0.71 each). The critic and its revisions did not, on these 40
  units, make English the judges rank higher.
- **The price:** the scholarly pipeline $0.043 a verse, Sonnet in one pass $0.005, flash $0.005 (BM1's own verses).
