## 4. What changed, and why

- **Translate All OCR'd** ran `advance_pipeline.py` (8 Sep): 22 texts named then, each ingested again from
  `data/raw` (Tesseract's pages) and translated with flash, `--min-quality 0.25`, whatever its path or the register
  says. Since then several have been repaired by vision (the Markandeya Purana is 100% vision), renamed or retired
  (`shiva_dhanur_veda`, `vasishtha_dhanur_veda`, `upapurana_nilamata_purana` are retired under new names: their old
  codes would have been ingested again as new texts), or withdrawn (the Bodhicaryavatara). One press could undo the
  path's work. `/api/pipeline/advance` now answers 410 and says where to go; the script stays, unrun.
- **Ingest, and Full's ingest:** `ingest_jsonl_fast.py` writes each passage over the one at the same page and place
  and keeps its English (by design: "translation is NOT overwritten on re-ingest"). Where the pages now read
  differently (a repair, a new OCR, a new segmentation), an English can stand beside a verse it was not made from.
  The ingest's own guard (INGEST_SOURCE_2026_10_04) already refuses Tesseract over a partial repair. For a text with
  English, the safe way is `reingest_pages.py` (REINGEST_2026_10_12: only the pages that changed; a passage whose
  text changed goes with its English, logged first, and comes back untranslated; the plan free, on the Repair page). So `/api/ingest` and `/api/queue/run` answer 409 with that reason for
  a text with English; the button asks and goes on with force. A new text, or one with no English, is as before.
- **Full** also asks the path's question (the guard of Start), since its third step translates.

Not changed: OCR, Classify, Embed, Entities, Export, the engines offered, `MT_ENGINE`.
