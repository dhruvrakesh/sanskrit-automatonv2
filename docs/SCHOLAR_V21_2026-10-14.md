## 2. SCHOLAR_V21_2026_10_14 (`scholar-v2.1`; the critic stays `scholar-critic-v2`)

**The passage is kept as written:**
- `clean_unit` keeps the draft's "translation" as the sentence's passage (`group_translation`), in the order
  English needs;
- each verse's row keeps its segment: the words of the passage that render it, which need not read in order;
- only a passage much shorter than its segments (no passage at all) gives way to them;
- the prompt's rule 10 says so: "the main clause may come first, as English wants it".

**Failures handled:**
- `Gemini.ask` asks again with room (16,384 tokens) when an answer cut off at the limit is not JSON, even when
  some text came back;
- a draft still not JSON is asked once more;
- `self.last` records how the answer ended, and a failed draft says it, for example "(it ended MAX_TOKENS, 31,204
  characters, not JSON)";
- a sentence whose draft fails is translated verse by verse ("split"), instead of failing all its verses.

**The price:** scholar-v2.1 is priced by the 20 scholar-v2 rows (about $0.012 a verse) until 10 of its own are
measured.

**The pages:**
- the rows are ordered by their passage's place now, since the prose's re-ingest moves the verses of page 1;
- a sentence whose passage is not its segments joined is shown whole after its verse range (`15–19`), with its
  segments in a fold, "Verse by verse";
- the prose view shows it the same way;
- scholar-v2's rows (their segments joined are their passage) are shown as before.

**The header:** it names the prompt (`SCHOLAR_V21_2026_10_14 (scholar-v2.1)`), and the summary counts the
sentences split.

**The importer:**
- it counts the appendix passages (`@`) as excluded, not "NOT READ";
- its Next lines name the `--out` directory, and give the reingest plan for a book already in the database.

## 3. READER_SOURCES_2026_10_14 (Srangam S2)

- **C17:** `public.corpus_reader_sources(p_doc)` counts one text's live passages by page and `ocr_engine`. It is
  read-only and behind the C5 gate (signed-in readers only; SECURITY DEFINER, search_path ''). It changes no
  other function. Rollback: `DROP FUNCTION public.corpus_reader_sources(text)`.
- **The reader:**
  - the corpus reader asks C17 once a text and gives each page the engine of most of its passages;
  - an e-text shows "Source: the BORI Critical Edition e-text (from GRETIL)", with no OCR confidence, and is never
    marked a hard-to-read scan;
  - a scan names its reader beside its confidence ("read from the scan by Tesseract OCR; OCR confidence 45%");
  - a passage without a recorded engine is as before.
- **Before C17 is pasted** the call fails and nothing changes.
- **The public reader** (/texts, srangam_text_passages) has no engine: unchanged.
- **Tested:** against PostgreSQL 16 (`tests/test_reader_sources_pg_2026_10_14.py`) and in Srangam
  (`src/__tests__/reader-sources.test.tsx`, 8; the whole Srangam suite as before; `tsc` clean; `vite build`).
