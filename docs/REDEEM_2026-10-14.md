## 2. REDEEM_2026_10_14

- **`scripts/redeem.py`** (new, read-only) puts each English verse in one class:
  - judged sound / judged wrong (the latest verdict of the judge, judge-v1);
  - vision (made from a vision reading);
  - no debris seen;
  - debris (8 or more Devanagari letters, and Latin letters among them: `repair_text.JUNK`).

  Each text gets one verdict:
  - **redeemed:** 97% or more kept, and the judge marks less than 10% of its sample;
  - **mostly redeemed:** half or more kept;
  - **to translate again:** less than half kept, and the scan is here;
  - **not redeemable as it is:** less than half kept, and the scan is not here;
  - **replaced:** paid for, and no English here now;
  - **withdrawn:** the register says so.

  It writes `data/redeem.json` (and, with `--html` or `--csv`, a page or a table). `--doc X` lists the passages the
  judge marks.
- **The dashboard** (after a restart):
  - each Library card has one line under it, in green, amber or red: how much of its English is kept, and what the
    rest needs. A card in "Not yet readable" whose English a better reading replaced says that too. Hover for
    the classes and the money;
  - the Library header counts the English kept, and links to `/redeem`;
  - `/redeem` has the ledger, verdict by verdict, and **Count again** (free, a minute; the step `redeem_all`);
  - the dashboard reads one small file, and reads it again only when it changes: no load on the Library.
- **The chart** (`text_path.py`): a text the judge marks (10% or more of its sample) has `retranslate_marked.py` as
  its NEXT, and the line to judge more.

## 3. T2_2026_10_14: the marked passages, translated again with the stronger engine

`scripts/retranslate_marked.py`:

- **Which passages:**
  - the latest English verdict is fidelity 2 or less (`--max-fidelity`);
  - the translation has not changed since the judge read it.
- **Left out, and listed:**
  - the judge blames the Sanskrit, or the Devanagari carries debris: the source first;
  - the texts withdrawn from the site.
- **A dry run by default:** free; nothing is called or written.
- **With `--yes`, for each passage:**
  1. `translate_passages.py --only-page P --only-idx I --retranslate --engine gemini:gemini-2.5-pro`. The old English
     goes to `translation_history`, as every re-translation does;
  2. the judge (judge_sample.py's prompt and model, judge-v1) reads the new English; its verdict replaces the old;
  3. the Hindi of a mended passage is made again (`--no-hindi` leaves it);
  4. the old and the new go to `data/retranslate/<stamp>_<doc>.jsonl`. A passage that is not mended is listed for a
     person to read.
- **It stops:**
  - before `--max-usd` (what usage_log says this run spent);
  - at the spend cap;
  - while the dashboard writes the same text.

  Without a key or the SDK it refuses before anything is written.
- **On the backup of 14 Oct:** 18 passages, about $0.030 (cap $0.10):
  - the Mallapurana 7, the Markandeya 5, the Shatapatha 4, the Sandilya 2;
  - 16 left out: 15 of the Bodhicaryavatara (withdrawn), and 1 with debris.

**Tests:** `tests/test_redeem_2026_10_14.py` (21). The patcher replays in `tests/test_read_in_2026_10_13.py` and
`tests/test_mirror_cap_2026_10_12.py` compare with this patch's backups. The full suite in the sandbox: 731 tests, the
same 69 failures and errors as before (none new).

## 4. Srangam (not changed in this release)

Srangam is at `d1752d4`, the same as origin. Its reader already shows each verse's OCR confidence (`quality_score`)
and the automatic translation check (`translation_qa`), in `src/components/reader/PassageBlock.tsx`.

The next steps, each a small change in its own release with its tests:

- **S1:** a mark on a verse whose Sanskrit carries OCR debris (the same test as `redeem.py`, in the browser, on the
  Sanskrit the reader already has): "the English is provisional: made from a misread page". No database change.
- **S2:** a reading mark (vision or Tesseract) from `corpus.passages.ocr_engine`, if the reader's
  `corpus_reader_page` returns it. The RUNBOOK's read-only SQL check says whether it does.
- **S3:** each text's verdict (redeemed, mostly, ...) on the Library cards, sent by the mirror. This needs a column or
  a table in `corpus`. It is designed when S1 and S2 are in.
