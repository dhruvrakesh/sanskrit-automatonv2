## 3. TRIAGE_2026_10_16 (scholar_translate.py)

**`triage_of(row)`** gives the tier of a row that did not pass:

| Tier | What it means |
|---|---|
| rejected | A person rejected it. |
| source | A source doubt: the printed text or its reading is in doubt. The source is checked, not the English. |
| serious | Any of these: the critic's answer was not read; the last critique (the critic's or the audit's) has a major issue; the judge gave fidelity 2 or less, or no answer; the second judge found a major error. |
| mild | The rest: the judge's fidelity 3; fluency below 4; a last critique "revise" with no major issue. |

A row a person accepted is not listed.

**The new flags:**
- **`--triage`** (with `--engine`; read-only and free): counts the tiers and their reasons, and writes
  `exports\scholar_triage_<doc>.csv`. The CSV is UTF-8 with a BOM, for Excel: one line a verse, with its tier,
  reasons, scores, judge, the Sanskrit, the English and the doubts.
- **`--only-flagged ENGINE [--serious]`:** a run reads again only the verses ENGINE did not pass (with `--serious`,
  the rejected and the serious).
  - A verse already read again by `--engine` since then is left (`--redo` reads it once more).
  - The context is every engine's rows.
- **`--status`:** adds, per engine, "not passed: N serious, M mild, K source".
- **The re-judge** prices a judge call as measured: usage_log, kind judge, since METERED_SINCE, 20 calls or more;
  otherwise $0.0027.

**The tiers today** (16 Oct backup, by the same rules):

| Text | Not passed | Serious | Source | Mild | Of them judged by Haiku |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Hayaśīrṣa | 242 | 84 | 22 | 136 | 8 serious, 25 mild |
| The Kāraṇāgama | 151 | 62 | 4 | 85 | — |

The largest reason is the flash judge's low fidelity:

The judge reads the Sanskrit and the English without the context (judge-v1). A second translation by another model,

## 5. THINKING_PROBE_2026_10_16 and the Gemini bill

**The levers:**
- Gemini 2.5 Flash takes a thinking budget, and 0 turns its thinking off. On the judge and the entities, the thinking
  is most of the cost.
- Turning it off changes what the model does, so it is measured before it is decided.
- `gemini_thinking_probe.py` judges N verses of a text's scholarly English twice, with the thinking and without it,
  through the REST API (generateContent, thinkingConfig.thinkingBudget 0).
- It prints both costs and how far the scores agree: the same fidelity, within one, the same pass or flag.
- It also says whether the installed google-generativeai SDK can carry a thinking budget, which decides how the change
  would be made.
- Every call is metered (kind "probe") and asks the spend cap. 12 verses cost about $0.04.

**Then a person decides, per kind:**

| Kind | The options |
|---|---|
| judge | thinking off; or gemini-2.5-flash-lite |
| entities | thinking off; flash-lite; or claude-haiku-5-5 on the credit, not built |
| working English | claude-haiku-5-5 or claude-sonnet-5-5@low on the credit, after the benchmark (CF3); thinking off is a risk to the translation |
