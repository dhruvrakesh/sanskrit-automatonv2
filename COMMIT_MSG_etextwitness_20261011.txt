- scripts/etext_witness.py (new): a GRETIL, sanskritdocuments.org or archive.org OCR text (Devanagari,
  IAST, Harvard-Kyoto, Velthuis, ITRANS) becomes a text of its own (<doc>__w_<site>): verses by their
  references, Devanagari, pages; measured against the scan page by page (six-letter n-grams of the
  consonant skeleton): how much it reads, in what order, and for each page the scan cannot read the
  witness's verses between its neighbours. Preview by default; --page N; --url (listed hosts) into
  sources\<doc>\; --write (data/raw_witness, data/witnesses.jsonl, the register: witness); --ingest
  (a backup, then ingest_jsonl_fast.py; the scan untouched); --list.
- scripts/patch_etext_witness_2026_10_11.py:
  repair_text.py: judge v2 (failed or short vision pages are no disagreement; five pages before the
    agreement sets the level; in texts cut into long passages the letters misread count, 2% material,
    1% moderate; --judge-all prints each kind); --release waits for a witness's translation;
  corpus_sync.py: a witness is not live (not mirrored, nor its pictures, nor the Corner's) until released;
  source_desk.py: a fetched OCR text's next step is etext_witness.py;
  dashboard.py: the repair page's Source desk lists the files fetched, takes a pasted e-text link, shows
    each witness (Ingest, under the translate lock; Translate; Adopt) and the pages the scan cannot
    read as the witness reads them; the Library's card of a witness;
  .gitignore: data/raw_witness/ and data/source_judged/ stay local (git rm -r --cached data/source_judged).
